# AXS Audit Methodology

AXS Audit is designed to identify digital barriers across websites, digital journeys and online platforms. The tool focuses on accessibility, usability and cognitive inclusion, with particular attention to disabled, neurodivergent and cognitively diverse users.

## Purpose

AXS Audit helps organisations understand:

- Where accessibility barriers exist
- How those barriers may affect users
- Which issues should be prioritised
- What practical remediation steps may be needed
- How digital experiences can become more inclusive

## Evaluation areas

AXS Audit may review areas including:

- Page structure
- Headings and landmarks
- Links and buttons
- Images and alternative text
- Forms and labels
- Keyboard access
- Colour contrast
- Text readability
- Content clarity
- Navigation consistency
- Cognitive load
- Error prevention and recovery
- User journey friction

## Accessibility and usability

AXS Audit is not limited to technical failures. It is intended to help organisations understand both formal accessibility issues and practical usability barriers that may affect real users.

## Cognitive inclusion

AXS Audit includes checks informed by W3C WAI cognitive accessibility guidance, including Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities.

This means the audit considers how digital experiences support users who may experience barriers related to:

- Attention
- Memory
- Reading and comprehension
- Planning and sequencing
- Language complexity
- Processing speed
- Sensory overload
- Decision fatigue

## Reporting approach

Findings should be presented in a way that helps teams act. Reports may include:

- Issue description
- User impact
- Relevant guidance or standard
- Priority level
- Affected pages or components
- Recommended remediation
- Suggested owner or team
- Evidence or screenshots where appropriate

## Limitations

Automated testing cannot identify every accessibility issue. AXS Audit should be used as part of a wider accessibility process that may include expert review, user testing, assistive technology testing and ongoing monitoring.
